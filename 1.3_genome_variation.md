## Human Genome Variation and Why It Matters

### Notation

Important to define which strand of DNA the SNP notation refers to. If we have:

`{A/G, T/C}`

Then the `A` and `T` refer to the same strand direction.

The fraction notation could mean one of at least three things:

*Reference/Alternate* where reference is the value in some human reference.
*Minor/Major* where minor is the less common allele (freq < 0.5).
*Ancestral/Derived* where ancestral is the allele present in a non human common
ancestor like great apes

### Catalog of Variants

- indels; 1/10th frequency of SNPs; 1-5 nucleotides in length
- STR (short tandem repeat); repeated sequences up to 6bp; replication slippage makes error prone
- VNTR (variable numbers of tandem repeats); ACAN gene: alleles can have 22-29x sequence motif
- large-scale variation / CNV (copy number variations); large deletions or duplications; average person carries 200+ deletions with median size of 7KB
- inversions; common one is 4.5MB around 50% global frequency
- complex structural variation; combination of everything

### SNP effects

Protein coding: synonymous, missense, stop/nonsense, frameshift, splice site
disruption
Gene regulation: quantitative shifts in protein abundance

Regulatory SNPs are main drivers of phenotypic variation and evolutionary
change

### Structural Variation (SV) effects

Coding sequence changes (relatively low frequency):
- ACAN
- Huntington's (STR repeat)

Copy number changes. Change expression in proportion to copies.
Some genes are *haploinsufficient*: single copy insufficient for function
Others are *copy-number sensitive*

AMY1 example of copy number evolving from selection. Enzyme for starch w/ 2-16
copies per person.

### Karyotypes evolve rapidly

Great apes have 24 pairs but next nearest relative, gibbons, have 26 pairs with
96 large chromosomal block rearrangements

Some think new centromeres evolve, irreversibly change product of meiosis and
spread through populations.

## Sequencing

1st Gen (Sanger) 1977 Fred Sanger + Walter Gilbert.
Soup of nucleotides with small fraction of capped dideoxy nucleotides that
block extension. Read out fragments on gel.

2nd Gen. Commercially available around 2006.
DNA fragmented and ligated to adapters in holes. Amplification occurs so each
hole has duplicated fragments. Sequencing by synthesis allows imaging of colony
one base pair at a time.

3rd Gen. 
Nanopore: electrical conductance of DNA molecule passing through
biologically-derived membrane.
PacBio: single molecule in well with type of sequencing by synthesis (RCA)

1st 800bp
2nd 2x150bp
3rd 1Mb

Places where mapping is amiguous (multiple matches): centromeres, subtelomeric,
ribosomal DNA clusters. All blocks of repeats.
10% of genome is "Unmappable" with short reads.

30X coverage considered gold standard. 100GB of data @ 3.1GB genome

### Basic Mutation calling

- SNPs: distinguish heterozygotes from sequencing errors. Frequency of error
small (0.1%)
- SVs: heterozygous deletion cuts depth to 50%. Read pairs span abnormally
large distance of chromosome

Exome sequencing uses only functional regions (1%)o
Commercial genotyping platforms: 500K - 2M SNPs

## Mutations

Typical cell suffers 70K lesions a day

- Adding/removing methyl groups, 
- Deamination + depurination
- pyrimidine dimers
- single and double strand breaks (55k vs 25 a day)

Typical cell between 10s and 100s of cell divisions
Trillions of total divisions

Repair and proofreading machinery are insanely effective
Germline mutation rates 1 per B per year

### Germline

Child born with 70. But this means only 0-1 in coding and handful in regualtory

- Mutation rate: 1.3 * 10 ** 8 or 1 in 100M bp
- 20-40 years; replicated hundreds of times

More mutations in older parents (achondroplasia; Willhelm Weinberg 1912)
So much so that the mutation count inherited can be largely explained by age of
parent.

More mutations in dads. Roughly 3x mutation rate. Across all mutation types (STRs).
Segregation errors mostly from meiosis errors in mothers.

Somatic mutations are more difficult to detect because you need low error rate
sequencing for single molecules.
